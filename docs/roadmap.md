# Roadmap

- Complete and publish results for the multi-device physical hardware and acoustic calibration matrix.
- Add an acoustic measurement workflow with a microphone and explicit permission.
- Evaluate process loopback as a separate source type with a clear privacy model. [Microsoft application loopback sample](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/samples/microsoft/windows-classic-samples/applicationloopbackaudio-sample/).
- Evaluate multistage resampling for extreme rate ratios and a configurable surround downmix after collecting hardware cases.
- Expand supported architectures and consider a signed installer after distribution and device verification.
