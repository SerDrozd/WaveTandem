# Code of conduct

Treat other contributors and users with respect. Give feedback on the work and provide enough context for others to reproduce an issue. Respect people's privacy and the differences in equipment, language, accessibility and experience they bring to the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the project community.

For conduct concerns, request a private contact channel from a maintainer. Share the relevant discussion and describe what happened without publishing private information.
